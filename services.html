Stage 1 & 2: Seatbelt Diffing & Entitlements

Starting from each non-(WebContent, GPU, Networking) iOS Seatbelt profile in Source/WebKit/Resources/SandboxProfiles/ios/ (at HEAD for active services and final pre-removal commits for retired services):

  • Differential Capability Matrix: Extracts every mach-lookup, iokit-open, filesystem path, sandbox extension, network rule, syscall, and lsopen grant, classifying whether it is Unique to Service (absent from WebContent, GPU, and Networking) or Elevated vs. WebContent.
  • Entitlements & Caller Gates: Audits Source/WebKit/Scripts/process-entitlements.sh for both the service’s own restricted entitlements and caller entitlements (e.g., com.apple.private.webkit.adattributiond, com.apple.private.webkit.webpush).

Stage 3: Source Code & IPC Call-Site Mapping

Maps every unique Seatbelt rule and entitlement back to its concrete C++/ObjC/Swift implementation in the WebKit repository:

  • Entry Points & Daemons: Traces XPCServiceEntryPoint, DaemonMain, and AuxiliaryProcessMain across WebAuthnProcess/, PrivateClickMeasurement/, webpushd/, ModelProcess/, and StorageProcess/.
  • IPC Surface (*.messages.in): Connects sandbox rules to the exact IPC message receivers and OS framework SPIs (e.g., ctkd, nfcd, apsd, CARenderServer, AppleKeyStoreUserClient).

Stage 4: Lifecycle & 3P BrowserEngineKit Impact

Evaluates why each service was created, how its sandbox evolved across all historical commits, and how it affects 3P browser competition on iOS:

  • 3 Active Services at HEAD: adattributiond (PCM daemon), webpushd (Web Push daemon), and Model (spatial 3D <model> process).
  • 2 Retired / Consolidated Services: WebAuthn (consolidated into OS AuthenticationServices.framework in May 2022) and Databases/Storage (merged into NetworkProcess in 2019–2020).
